# Investigation Design Principles

---

# Core Principle

The player forms beliefs.

The player does not discover objective truth.

---

# Investigation Model

Evidence
↓
Interpretation
↓
Belief

---

# Conversation Rules

Conversations are not exhaustive.

Players should never gain all available information through dialogue completion alone.

---

# Information Costs

Information may require:

- Trust
- Awareness
- Evidence
- Timing
- Reputation

---

# Dead Ends

Dead ends are permitted.

Dead ends should feel meaningful.

Dead ends should not permanently halt progress.

---

# Contradictions

Contradictions are content.

Contradictions are not bugs.

---

# Awareness

Awareness should complicate understanding.

Awareness should not function as a simple upgrade path.

---

# Standing

Social consequences create new paths.

Social consequences close some paths.

Social consequences should remain recoverable.

---

# Beliefs

Players may form beliefs.

The game should not confirm which belief is correct.

Multiple interpretations should remain viable whenever possible.
