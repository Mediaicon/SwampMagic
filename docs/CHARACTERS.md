# Swamp Magic Characters

This document tracks character intent, narrative purpose, social role, and design constraints.

---

# Ferryman

## Role

Gatekeeper

## Narrative Purpose

Introduces uncertainty.

Represents the idea that Ferry Landing may be more than it appears.

Exists to plant questions rather than answer them.

## Themes

- Consequences
- Regret
- Silence
- Hidden knowledge

## Social Style

Respects:
- Directness
- Persistence
- Curiosity

Dislikes:
- Manipulation
- Empty conversation

## Knowledge Level

High

The Ferryman knows more than he is willing to share.

## Protected Mystery Rules

The Ferryman may imply.

The Ferryman may suggest.

The Ferryman may warn.

The Ferryman may NEVER explicitly answer a Protected Mystery.

# Storekeeper

## Role

Information Hub

## Narrative Purpose

Represents practical reality.

Acts as a counterweight to the Ferryman.

Introduces alternative explanations.

## Themes

- Community
- Rumor
- Observation

## Social Style

Respects:
- Courtesy
- Reciprocity

Dislikes:
- Intrusive questioning
- Arrogance

## Knowledge Level

Medium

Knows everyone's business.
Knows very little of the truth.

# Old Woman

## Role

Keeper of Memory

## Narrative Purpose

Represents history, folklore, and narrative uncertainty.

## Themes

- Memory
- Myth
- Survival

## Social Style

Responds to stories more readily than facts.

## Knowledge Level

Variable

Sometimes knows exactly what happened.

Sometimes tells the story she wishes had happened.
