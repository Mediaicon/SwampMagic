# Concept Dictionary

This document defines the meaning of core terms used throughout the Swamp Magic project.

These definitions are authoritative for design, implementation, documentation, and AI review.

When a term is used in design discussions, documentation, or code comments, it should align with the definitions below.

---

# Documentation Authority

CONCEPT_DICTIONARY.md is authoritative for terminology.

Subsystem documents are authoritative for subsystem behavior.

Any new capitalized game term must be added to CONCEPT_DICTIONARY.md.

When terminology conflicts with subsystem documentation, the Concept Dictionary takes precedence.

---

# Awareness

Awareness represents a growing ability to notice patterns, contradictions, hidden details, and inconsistencies.

Awareness is a burden.

Awareness is not a power progression system.

Higher Awareness should often complicate understanding rather than simplify it.

Awareness may reveal:
- Additional observations
- Alternate interpretations
- Hidden clues
- Contradictions

Awareness may never:
- Reveal objective truth automatically
- Resolve a Protected Mystery

---

# Belief

A belief is an interpretation formed by the player.

Beliefs may be supported by evidence.

Beliefs may be contradicted by evidence.

Beliefs are not inherently true.

Multiple players may reach different beliefs using the same information.

The game should rarely confirm whether a belief is correct.

---

# Condition

A Condition is a state currently affecting the character.

Conditions may be temporary or long-term.

Examples:

- Awareness
- Injury
- Fatigue
- Reputation
- Standing

Not all Conditions change at the same rate.

Some Conditions (such as Injury or Fatigue) may fluctuate rapidly.

Others (such as Awareness) may develop slowly over the course of the game.

---

# Truth

Truth is objective reality.

Truth may be unavailable.

Truth may be unknowable.

Protected Mysteries are intentionally separated from objective truth.

Most investigations are designed to produce beliefs rather than truth.

---

# Protected Mystery

A Protected Mystery is a question that may never be definitively answered.

Players may investigate Protected Mysteries.

Players may form beliefs regarding Protected Mysteries.

NPCs may hold opinions regarding Protected Mysteries.

The game may not reveal objective truth regarding a Protected Mystery.

Protected Mysteries exist to preserve uncertainty.

---

# Personal Mystery

A Personal Mystery is a player-origin investigation.

Personal Mysteries begin during character creation.

Personal Mysteries are intended to reach meaningful conclusions.

Resolving a Personal Mystery may create additional questions.

Personal Mysteries may connect to Protected Mysteries without resolving them.

---

# Question

A Question is an individual investigative topic.

Questions generate uncertainty.

Questions produce evidence, interpretations, and beliefs.

Examples:

- Why did the Original Preacher leave?
- Who owns the burned ledger?
- Why was the boat abandoned?

Questions combine to form Cases.

---

# Case

A Case is a collection of related Questions.

Cases provide structure for investigations.

A Case may contain:
- Multiple Questions
- Multiple Clues
- Contradictory testimony
- Physical evidence

Cases generally end with the player forming one or more beliefs.

---

# Clue

A Clue is a discoverable piece of information.

Clues may be:
- Physical
- Social
- Historical
- Environmental

A Clue is not automatically evidence.

A Clue only becomes meaningful when interpreted.

---

# Evidence

Evidence is information the player considers relevant to a Question or Case.

Evidence may support:
- Multiple interpretations
- Contradictory interpretations

Evidence does not inherently prove anything.

Different players may draw different conclusions from identical evidence.

---

# Interpretation

An Interpretation is a proposed explanation for evidence.

Interpretations sit between evidence and belief.

Multiple interpretations may exist simultaneously.

Interpretations should frequently compete with one another.

---

# Contradiction

A Contradiction occurs when two or more explanations, claims, memories, observations, or interpretations cannot comfortably coexist.

Contradictions are content.

Contradictions are not bugs.

Contradictions are intended to create investigation opportunities.

A contradiction does not automatically indicate a lie.

---

# Trust

Trust represents the relationship between the player and a specific individual.

Trust is personal.

Trust is not public.

Trust may influence:
- Conversation options
- Information access
- Cooperation

Trust may increase or decrease through player actions.

Trust should generally be recoverable.

---

# Standing

Standing represents the player's social position within a community.

Standing is broader than Trust.

Standing is influenced by:
- Reputation
- Associations
- Public actions
- Rumors

Standing is generally hidden from the player.

Players should feel Standing through NPC reactions rather than numerical values.

---

# Reputation

Reputation is what people believe about the player.

Reputation may be accurate or inaccurate.

Reputation spreads through:
- Observation
- Rumor
- Gossip
- Public actions

Reputation influences Standing.

---

# Association

Association is the act of being connected with a person, group, or belief.

NPCs notice associations.

Association may affect Standing and Trust.

Examples:

- Spending time with the New Preacher
- Frequently assisting the Ferryman
- Publicly supporting a belief

Associations should have social consequences.

---

# Relationship

A Relationship is an ongoing social connection between two people.

Relationships may include:
- Mutual trust
- Respect
- Fear
- Suspicion
- Obligation

Relationships are dynamic.

Damaged Relationships should create complications rather than permanent lockouts.

---

# Reputation Recovery

Reputation Recovery is the process of repairing social damage.

Recovery may occur through:
- Time
- Favors
- Assistance
- Shared information
- Solving community problems

Recovery should create gameplay opportunities.

Recovery should not feel like punishment removal.

---

# Dead End

A Dead End is an investigative path that does not immediately produce useful information.

Dead Ends are acceptable.

Dead Ends should:
- Provide context
- Reinforce atmosphere
- Redirect investigation

Dead Ends should not create unwinnable states.

---

# Missable Information

Missable Information is content the player may never discover.

Missing information should modify understanding.

Missing information should not usually prevent completion of a Case.

---

# Investigation

An Investigation is the process of gathering information, evaluating interpretations, and forming beliefs.

The investigation model is:

Evidence
→ Interpretation
→ Belief

An Investigation should rarely function as a simple sequence of correct answers.

---

# Origin

An Origin is a character background selected during character creation.

Origins are backgrounds, not classes.

Origins shape:
- Personal Mysteries
- Starting relationships
- Starting perspective
- Roleplaying opportunities

Origins should not lock players out of core content.

---

# Design Authority

The Design Authority is the highest narrative authority for the project.

All content, systems, mechanics, and lore should comply with the Design Authority.

When conflict exists between implementation and the Design Authority, the Design Authority takes precedence.

---

# Canon

Canon represents accepted truth within project documentation.

Canon should be recorded in project documentation.

Canon should not be assumed from memory.

Canon may contain:
- Confirmed facts
- Historical events
- Character details

Canon should never contain answers to Protected Mysteries.

---

## Brief

Brief is an informal or legacy term for a Case.

Within the project, Brief and Case may be used interchangeably unless explicitly stated otherwise.

For consistency, new documentation should prefer the term Case.

Existing references to Briefs should generally be interpreted as Cases, Case Assignments, or investigative work provided to the player.

Briefs follow all rules governing Cases.

---

# Core Principle

The player does not complete uncertainty.

The player learns to carry it.
