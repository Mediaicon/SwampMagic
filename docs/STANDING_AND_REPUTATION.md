# Standing and Reputation

Standing is hidden from the player.

Players never see numerical values.

---

# Core Principle

Consequences are informational.

Consequences are social.

Consequences are recoverable.

Consequences are not permanent lockouts.

---

# NPC Reactions

NPCs may react to:

- Observed actions
- Rumors
- Friendships
- Enemies
- Investigative choices
- Public beliefs

---

# Association

People notice who you associate with.

Example:

Frequent contact with the New Preacher may improve standing with supporters and reduce standing with opponents.

The player will never see numerical changes.

Reputation should be felt through conversation and behavior.

---

# Recovery

Damaged relationships are recoverable.

Recovery methods may include:

- Time
- Favors
- Assistance
- Shared information
- Solving problems
- Community actions

---

# Design Rule

Bad decisions should create complications.

Bad decisions should not create unwinnable states.
