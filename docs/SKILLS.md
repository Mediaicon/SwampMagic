## Skills

This document defines the core skills used by player characters in Swamp Magic.

Skills represent what a character has learned to do.

Skills are distinct from Attributes, which represent who the character is, and Conditions, which represent what is currently happening to the character.

Skills should improve through experience, training, investigation, relationships, and participation in the world.

Skills should remain useful throughout the entire game.

No skill should become a universal solution.

---

# Design Principles

Skills should:

- Support investigation.
- Support roleplaying.
- Support multiple playstyles.
- Create meaningful character identity.
- Remain useful throughout the game.

Skills should rarely act as binary pass/fail gates.

Instead, skills should:

- Change how information is discovered.
- Change how information is interpreted.
- Change how NPCs respond.
- Change what opportunities become available.

Failure should usually create complications rather than stop progress.

# Rapport

## Purpose

Rapport represents a character's ability to build relationships and gain trust.

## Influenced By

- Presence
- Will

## Examples

Rapport may be used for:

- Building trust
- Recovering damaged relationships
- Earning confidence
- Making introductions
- Gathering information through conversation
- Negotiation

## Does Not Do

Rapport does not:

- Mind control NPCs
- Force agreement
- Automatically reveal secrets

Trust must still be earned.

---

# Lore

## Purpose

Lore represents a character's ability to understand history, culture, traditions, myths, and local knowledge.

## Influenced By

- Knowledge
- Will

## Examples

Lore may be used for:

- Understanding local customs
- Recognizing symbols
- Identifying historical references
- Interpreting stories and folklore
- Understanding religious practices

## Does Not Do

Lore does not:

- Reveal objective truth
- Resolve Protected Mysteries
- Replace investigation

Knowledge provides context, not answers.

---

# Investigation

## Purpose

Investigation represents a character's ability to gather, organize, compare, and interpret information.

## Influenced By

- Knowledge
- Presence

## Examples

Investigation may be used for:

- Comparing evidence
- Finding inconsistencies
- Identifying contradictions
- Reviewing records
- Following leads
- Building theories

## Does Not Do

Investigation does not:

- Automatically determine truth
- Reveal hidden answers
- Eliminate uncertainty

Investigation produces interpretations, not certainty.

# Survival

## Purpose

Survival represents a character's ability to function in difficult environments.

## Influenced By

- Fortitude
- Knowledge

## Examples

Survival may be used for:

- Travel
- Enduring weather
- Recognizing environmental hazards
- Finding safe routes
- Navigating difficult terrain
- Living off the land

## Does Not Do

Survival does not:

- Grant combat superiority
- Replace Fortitude
- Eliminate environmental costs

Survival helps a character manage danger, not ignore it.

## Influenced By

# Resolve

## Purpose

Resolve represents a character's ability to continue despite hardship.

## Influenced By

- Will
- Fortitude

## Examples

Resolve may be used for:

- Managing stress
- Resisting fear
- Continuing investigations
- Enduring difficult situations
- Confronting uncomfortable truths
- Maintaining focus

## Does Not Do

Resolve does not:

- Prevent Awareness
- Eliminate consequences
- Remove emotional impact

---

# Skill Growth

Skills improve through use and experience.

Examples include:

- Completing investigations
- Building relationships
- Studying
- Practicing
- Solving problems
- Surviving difficult situations

Growth should reflect a character's lived experiences.

---

# Origins and Skills

Origins are backgrounds, not classes.

Origins may provide:

- Starting skill bonuses
- Familiarity with certain skills
- Access to unique opportunities
- Specialized knowledge

Origins should not permanently restrict skill development.

Any character should eventually be capable of learning any skill.

---

# Skills and Protected Mysteries

No skill may bypass a Protected Mystery.

A skill may:

- Reveal information
- Provide context
- Change interpretation
- Generate new questions

A skill may not:

- Reveal objective truth regarding a Protected Mystery
- Resolve uncertainty that has been intentionally protected by Design Authority

Skills improve a character's ability to investigate.

They do not grant certainty.

# Future Skills

These skills are reserved for future discussion.

- Firearms

# Core Principle

Skills influence how a character experiences the world.

Skills should change perspective, opportunity, and interpretation.

Skills should not function as simple keys that unlock answers.
