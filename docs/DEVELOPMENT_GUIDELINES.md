Conversation Rules

1. Not every conversation rewards the player.

2. Information may be missed.

3. Asking at the wrong time matters.

4. Trust changes access.

5. Awareness changes interpretation.

6. NPCs know more than they say.

7. Contradictions are content.

8. The player should never obtain all information simply by exhausting dialogue.

Investigation Design Rules

Information should rarely be permanently lost.

Consequences should redirect investigations rather than end them.

Bad decisions create:
- new obligations
- social complications
- alternative paths

They should not create unwinnable states.

Conversations are not exhaustive.

A player should never obtain all available information
simply by selecting every dialogue option.

Relationships are dynamic.

Trust damaged through conversation may be repaired
through time, favors, evidence, social action,
or other investigation activities.

Villages remember.

NPCs may react to:
- who the player associates with
- who the player helps
- public actions
- rumors
- stated beliefs

These reactions should be communicated through the world
rather than explicit reputation meters.
